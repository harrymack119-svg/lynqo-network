Refund Policy

Refunds for digital products on Lynqo

This Refund Policy explains when customers may be eligible for a refund, the conditions for digital products, and how refunds are handled when different sellers use different policies.

1. When refunds may be available

Refunds may be available when a product is defective, not delivered as described, or otherwise fails to meet the seller’s published expectations. Customers should review the product details and seller information before purchasing.

2. Digital products

For digital products, refunds are generally not available once access has been provided and the customer has downloaded or used the product, unless there is a clear issue such as a broken file, incorrect delivery, or a violation of the seller’s stated terms.

3. Different seller policies

Some sellers on Lynqo may set their own refund conditions. In those cases, the seller’s policy on the product page will apply, and customers should read it carefully before completing a purchase.

4. How to request a refund

If you believe you are eligible for a refund, please contact the seller through the product page or reach out to Lynqo support with your order details so we can review the request.
